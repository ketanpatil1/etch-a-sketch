Welcome to Etch-A-Sketch!

Hover your mouse over the grid to draw whatever comes to your mind!

Here's how it works:

  • Click or press d to enter draw mode.
    • You can also press and hold Ctrl while moving the cursor to draw.
  • Click or press e to enter erase mode.
    • You can also press and hold Shift while moving the cursor to erase.
  • Click or press m to enter move mode.
  • Click or press r to refresh the grid.
  • Click or press i to invert the colors.
  • Click or press ? to open this dialog.
